About

I got into data analytics the way most analysts do — by noticing a pattern no one had pointed out yet, and wanting to prove it with numbers rather than a hunch. That instinct is now the throughline of everything I build.

My toolkit is Python (pandas, seaborn, SciPy, NumPy) for analysis, SQL for querying, and Google Looker Studio for turning findings into dashboards people can actually use. During my internship at Cognifyz Technologies, I cleaned and analyzed a 9,500-row restaurant dataset — chain detection, price-to-rating correlation, geographic clustering — the kind of work that doesn't make headlines but changes decisions.
